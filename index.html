DEEP LEARNING · DERIVATIVES · 2026
Independent project · after Buehler et al., Deep Hedging (2019)

Deep Hedging of Options

  • Trained a neural network in PyTorch to hedge a short option under transaction costs and Heston stochastic volatility (calibrated to SPX options), minimising the 95% tail loss (CVaR).
  • On 100,000 unseen paths it cut tail loss by 33% versus Heston delta plus a variance swap, and by 4–11% versus delta hedges with a tuned no-trade band.
  • With costs it learned to trade less (1.06 vs 2.27 shares per path at 20 bp) and held up out of sample on 16 years of real NIFTY 50 paths.

DERIVATIVES PRICING · 2026
Independent project · after Heston (1993)

Heston Pricing Engine

  • Built the Heston stochastic volatility model from scratch with three independent pricing families (Fourier, Monte Carlo, PDE with ADI), each checked against the others and published benchmarks.
  • The COS method prices to 8e-8 in 0.15 ms, about 10,000x faster than Monte Carlo; Andersen's QE scheme removes Euler's variance bias.
  • Calibrated to 1,880 real SPX quotes across 13 expiries with 0.90 vol points RMSE, and showed where the model fails: the steep skew of short-dated options.

ML · EQUITIES · 2026
Independent project · after López de Prado (2018)

Nifty ML Alpha: Does the Backtest Leak?

  • Ranked 98 Nifty 100 stocks by next-month return with LightGBM and ridge on 15 price and volume features, 2010–2026, as a long-short portfolio net of 15 bps costs.
  • Shuffled K-fold inflated the Sharpe ratio about 7x (3.68 vs 0.53); purged, embargoed K-fold and walk-forward agree on the honest number.
  • A placebo on random-walk stocks proved it: naive K-fold found a Sharpe of 5 in pure noise, the leak-free methods found nothing. Out of sample the simple ridge model won.
