Selected work

Volatility Dynamics and the Fear Index: Nifty 50 and India VIX

2026 · Team project, Financial Derivatives and Risk Management
  • Analysed 1,536 days of Nifty 50 and India VIX (2020–26). Implied volatility exceeded realised on 78% of days (Newey-West t = 2.8), a persistent variance risk premium.
  • Re-implemented NSE's VIX formula from option prices, then split the market into low, normal and high fear regimes, including the June 2024 election shock.
  • Backtested Black-Scholes-priced protective puts. A VIX-timed hedge cut maximum drawdown from −38% to −22%, most of it in the March 2020 crash.

Landslide Susceptibility Mapping with GIS and Machine Learning

2026 · B.Tech project under Prof. Susmita Panda
  • Built a landslide-risk model on 4,431 events and 14 satellite-derived factors in Hamirpur, Himachal Pradesh, comparing kNN, SVM, ANN, Random Forest and XGBoost.
  • Showed that random splits leak information (XGBoost AUC 0.97 vs 0.85 on held-out 5 km blocks), the same trap as look-ahead bias in a backtest. Tuned on spatial cross-validation; the final SVM scored AUC 0.905 on unseen regions.
